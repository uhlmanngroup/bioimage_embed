Biological image analysis increasingly relies on learned representations for downstream tasks such as classification, segmentation, and drug discovery. However, standardized tools for training and deploying autoencoders on microscopy data are lacking, and domain-specific preprocessing requirements pose significant barriers to adoption. We present \texttt{bioimage\_embed}, a modular framework that combines domain-specific preprocessing for microscopy formats (TIFF, OME-NGFF), flexible autoencoder architectures (VAE, VQVAE, MAE), and modern machine learning engineering practices including type-safe configurations and reproducible checkpointing. The framework integrates with the HuggingFace ecosystem for model sharing and provides a command-line interface accessible to non-programmers. We demonstrate the utility of \texttt{bioimage\_embed} on standard bioimage datasets including Cell Painting and Image Data Resource collections, achieving competitive reconstruction quality and downstream task performance. Our open-source library provides a standardized pipeline for bioimage representation learning, enabling reproducible research and facilitating adoption of deep learning methods in computational biology.

\textbf{Keywords:} deep learning, autoencoders, biological imaging, microscopy, representation learning, VAE, VQVAE
